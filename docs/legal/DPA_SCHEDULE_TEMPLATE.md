# Data processing agreement: processing schedule (template)

To attach to the agreement between [University] (controller) and [Operator] (processor) under UK GDPR Article 28.
Not legal advice; have your legal team complete and review it.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Subject matter | Provision of the Offerpath careers platform to the controller's students and careers staff |
| Duration | Term of the services agreement, plus up to 30 days for deletion and backup expiry |
| Nature and purpose | Storing and analysing CVs, cover letters and job adverts to give feedback; tracking applications; aggregate reporting to the careers service |
| Personal data | Name, university email, student number, course, year, faculty, fee status, CV and cover letter content, scores, application tracking, preferences |
| Data subjects | Students and graduates of the controller; careers staff |
| Sub-processors | As listed at `/legal/sub-processors`; 30 days' notice of changes with a right to object |
| Location | Primary storage UK/EU; transfers to the US for AI processing under the UK IDTA |
| Security | Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest; row-level access control; least-privilege service keys; per-user rate and usage limits; logging without CV content |
| Assistance | Self-service export and deletion for data subject requests; breach notification without undue delay and within 48 hours of becoming aware |
| End of contract | Deletion of all controller data within 30 days, or return in a standard format on request |
| Audit | Annual security summary on request; reasonable audit rights on notice |
